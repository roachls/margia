# Rules

1. If you are still playing the last note, wait until you are done.
1. If your queue is empty, roll 2d6. If you roll <= 4, play a random note.
1. Take a note off your queue. Roll 2d5:
<ul>
  <li>1: Lower velocity by 10</li>
  <li>2: Down an octave</li>
  <li>3: Repeat your last note.</li>
  <li>4: Pause 1 beat. Make your last note the note from the queue.</li>
  <li>5: Repeat the note from the queue, but up a 4th.</li>
  <li>6: Repeat the note from your queue.</li>
  <li>7: Repeat the note from the queue, but up a minor 6th.</li>
  <li>8: Put the note back on the queue.</li>
  <li>9: Up an octave.</li>
  <li>10: raise velocity by 10</li>
</ul>
