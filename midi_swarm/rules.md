# Rules

1. If you are still playing the last note, wait until you are done.
1. If your queue is empty, pause one beat and then roll 2d6. If you roll <= 4, play a random note.
1. Take a note off your queue. Roll 1d10:
  a. 1: Repeat the note from your queue.
  a. 2: Repeat your last note.
  a. 3: Pause 1 beat. Make your last note the note from the queue.
  d. 4: Repeat the note from the queue, but up a 4th.
  e. 5: Repeat the note from the queue, but up a minor 6th.
  f. 6: Pause 2 beats and put the note back on the queue.
  g. 7: Up an octave.
  h: 8: Down an octave
  i: 9: Lower velocity by 10%
  j: 10: raise velocity by 10%
